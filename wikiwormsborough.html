Wormsborough


Wormsborough is a city in the East Riding of Yorkshire, England, located on the Holderness Part. The city is about 4 miles south of Bridlington and 30 miles east of York, at the mouth of the river Tynd, which flows into the North Sea on the east end of the city.

Wormsborough has a very dense cape population, with several villains who generally stay inside their own territories. The city's main source of income used to be fishing, but in recent years the council has pivoted heavily towards cape tourism.

History

Ancient History

Archaeological digs have found spear tips and bone fragments in the woods outside of the city, showing evidence of prehistoric human activity dating back approximately 2,000 years. The mouth of the river does not seem to have had any particular cultural or spiritual importance, though there are some standing stones in the north end of Heatherroot park in areas inaccessible to the public.

The mouth of the river was used as a landing port for scandinavian colonists to Great Britain, who built the starts of the city that stands today.

The town flourised as a port for fish and shellfish for many years, but in recent years the industry has moved elsewhere and demand for Wormsborough's export has dwindled.

Hull Refugees and rebuilding

In 2001 when Hull was destroyed in an attack by Leviathan, much of the population fled to nearby cities. One such place was Wormsborough. Due to the high number of refugees, the town began rapidly expanding, building council housing around the north end and on the south side of the river, and the town was promoted to a city.

This caused unrest among the population, as the previously small fishing town was expanding greatly to fit in the new population. Several protests were launched and became violent, ending with the deaths of two refugees and a citizen of the city in 2004.

Current Era

With the flood of refugees came a high proportion of new capes, resulting in a city with an exorbitant amount of new capes. Amass was one of the first new capes in town. She claims that she came from Hull, but was never publicly active there. She was swiftly followed by several heroes and villains that had not been seen before and some who had already been active elsewhere, marking Wormsborough as the place to be for capes in East Yorkshire.

Capes

Wormsborough's villain territories, as of 2011

Wormsborough has a small hero population compared to its villains, but as its villains do not usually form gangs or cause chaos they are not required to handle all of them all of the time. Wormsborough is very heavily divided into different villains' territories, with most conflicts between the groups happening at the borders.
