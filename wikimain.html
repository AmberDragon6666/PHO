Parahumans Online
Parahumans Online (or PHO for short) is the "go-to" website for capes and related news. We do our best to stay up to date on international news about parahumans with specific boards being used for local news. Our website includes a wiki along with message boards, chat rooms and information regarding cape groups and events. Since we are a collaborative community, that means anyone (including you), can build and expand our wiki. We depend on readers like you to get involved and add content. Feel free to add new pages or edit existing ones. However, bear in mind that we are moderated by a number of official PRO admins as well as a number of site moderators. You may face a temporary suspension or even permanent ban for breaking our community guidelines.
