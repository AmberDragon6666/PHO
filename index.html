Shadow Stalker New Probationary Member Of The Brockton Bay Wards

30/07/2010

Shadow Stalker, controversial Brockton Bay vigilante, has been inducted into the Wards program on probation. She is presently in San Diego, learning how to work within a superhero team.

D.D.I.D Revoked In Madison

28/07/2010

A year after the area was quarantined due to an attack, the controversial D.D.I.D act has been revoked. This means that those who leave the quarantine zone no longer need to be marked with a bird tattoo to show their potential exposure.

"Panacea" Amy Dallon Latest Member Of New Wave

23/01/2010

New Wave, the no-secret-identities family and hero team of Brockton Bay, is welcoming their newest member. How she will fare on the field is yet to be seen, but the people of Brockton Bay are hopeful.
