Amass


Amass is a hero in Wormsborough. She has been operating for the last 5 years as one of the first heroes to arrive in the city after Hull's destruction. She is second-in-command of the wormsborough Protectorate, and has been since 2007.

Power

Amass has the power to pause objects that she's touching, making them stop and become immovable. She can unfreeze them at any time, redirecting any energy they have accumulated in the time they were paused. This includes heat, gravity, and any kinetic energy. She can also convert energy into other types, such as converting heat into momentum or vice versa.

In action, she uses this to charge projectiles, launching them from oblique angles at unpredictable times. She can also stop herself in mid-air by pausing her clothes and shoes, leaping from nothing by redirecting the accumulated gravitational energy.

Career

History

Amass has been operating in Wormsborough since mid-2006. She is open about having come from Hull, where she survived the attack, and is pro-refugees. This has led to her being controversial to some of the natives of the city, but embraced by the refugee population. She participated in calming down the riots and kept the supervillains of the city from causing further chaos that would incite even more violence from the public.

She took Ultraviolet's position as second-in-command of the wormsborough protectorate after his death, and has remained in that position until this day. She helped to clear the north end of supervillains so the PRT could build their HQ in the city.

Personality

As the second-in-command of the wormsborough protectorate, Amass makes a lot of public appearances. She is present at most PRT events.

In combat, Amass rarely quips, preferring to dispatch her enemy efficiently. She may gloat after bringing them into custody, however. She was briefly famous after a video clip from a local news station went viral. In the clip, Amass was celebrating that Clog had been imprisoned after an altercation but was interrupted mid-sentence by an explosion behind her as the supervillain escaped. Since then, Amass has waited longer before announcing that villains have been captured.
